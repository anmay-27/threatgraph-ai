# Investigation studio artwork

Created using the built-in imagegen tool. These illustrations are decorative concepts, not investigation evidence. Original generated PNGs were copied into the project without modification.

## Hero

Saved asset: `frontend/public/art/investigation-hero.png`

Prompt:

Create a premium 3D editorial illustration for the hero of ThreatGraph, a digital fraud investigation web application. Landscape 3:2 composition. A sculptural translucent mint glass magnifying lens hovering over an ivory document, a floating abstract photographic portrait card, and a small dark browser window, connected by fine curved mint cables and tiny amber nodes on a deep midnight navy studio background. Sophisticated tactile frosted glass, soft porcelain, brushed metal, rich realistic shadows, restrained mint and warm amber lighting. Main composition centered with generous breathing room, strong readable silhouette at 500px wide. No text, no letters, no digits, no logos, no padlocks, no hooded hackers, no stock cyber matrix, no UI screenshot. Beautiful product illustration, refined art direction, subtle grain, high quality rendered imagery. This is decorative concept art, not a depiction of real investigation evidence.

## Case illustration

Saved asset: `frontend/public/art/offer-investigation.png`

Prompt:

Premium editorial 3D still life for a digital fraud investigation app's case-study card. Landscape 3:2. On a warm ivory paper background: a midnight navy open envelope holding an ivory employment offer sheet, a mint translucent small magnifying lens and three tiny brass connected evidence nodes. Beautiful tactile paper folds, elegant minimal arrangement, soft natural side lighting and gentle shadows, highly polished product photography of sculptural objects. Restrained midnight navy, mint green, ivory and amber palette. Objects centered with generous margins. No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no fake UI, no currency symbols. Decorative conceptual illustration of checking a suspicious job offer, not evidence.

## Integration and review

`visual-studio.css` provides a split desktop hero, ivory/mint palette, two-column submission area and compact illustrated demo card. Mobile stacks the hero and keeps the case illustration compact. Images have reserved dimensions; the secondary image loads lazily. Browser checks exercise image loading, hero navigation, report navigation, filters and mobile layout. Review captures are in `research/results/frontend-experience`.
